# Bank Customer Churn Analysis

## Introduction
Customer retention is a critical metric for financial institutions. This project analyzes a dataset of 10,000 bank customer records to identify the demographic and behavioral trends driving account closures.

## Problem Statement
The bank is experiencing an unexplained volume of customer attrition. The objective of this analysis is to systematically explore the data to identify key patterns and build an interactive dashboard that allows stakeholders to pinpoint high-risk customer segments.

## Tools Used
* **Microsoft Excel:** Data Cleaning, Pivot Tables, Data Modeling, Dashboard Visualization.
* **AI Assistance:** utilized for report formatting and data interpretation structuring.

## Data Transformation and Cleaning
Before analysis, the raw data was audited and prepared to ensure accuracy. 
* Checked for and removed duplicate entries.
* Formatted currency and categorical columns for consistency.
* Validated empty fields to ensure clean aggregation for the Pivot Tables.

## Insights and Findings
* **Overall Attrition:** The bank is experiencing a 20.4% total customer churn rate.
* **Geographic Risk:** While France holds the highest volume of customers, Germany presents a disproportionate risk, showing a significantly higher rate of churn relative to its size.
* **Activity Gap:** Inactive customers are abandoning their accounts at a severely elevated volume (1,302) compared to active members (735).
* **Gender Variance:** Female customers are closing their accounts at a noticeably higher rate than male customers.

## Recommendations
1. **Targeted Re-engagement:** Launch automated re-engagement campaigns specifically tailored to inactive users before they reach the critical churn threshold.
2. **Geographic Investigation:** Conduct a localized audit of German branch operations and competitor offers, as the current retention strategy is underperforming in that specific market.
