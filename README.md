# Bank-Customer-Churn
An interactive Excel dashboard analyzing 10,000 bank customer records to identify churn trends
**What I Did:** I analyzed 10,000 bank customer records in Excel to figure out why people were closing their accounts. 
**How I Did It:** I cleaned the raw data, used Pivot Tables, and built a fully interactive dashboard with clickable filters.
**What I Found Out:**
* The bank is losing about 20% of its customers overall.
* Inactive customers are closing their accounts at nearly double the rate of active ones.
* Even though France has the most customers, Germany has a major problem keeping theirs.
* More women are leaving the bank compared to men.
